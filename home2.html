Frequently Asked Questions

How long are OSHA certification cards valid?

OSHA outreach training cards do not officially expire. However, many construction companies, industrial sites, and state authorities require employees to complete refresher training every 3 to 5 years to keep compliance current.

Can training sessions be customized for specific facility hazards?

Yes! We specialize in tailored compliance training. Our outreach trainers will perform a pre-course review of your facility's hazard profile and incorporate your site-specific safety plans directly into the curriculum.

Do you offer discounts for bulk team enrollments?

Yes, we provide tiered pricing and flat-rate onsite group options for teams of 10 or more. You can check pricing and request a formal quote on our Bulk Enrollment page.
