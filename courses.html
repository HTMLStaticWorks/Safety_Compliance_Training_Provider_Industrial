Frequently Asked Questions

Can I complete training on mobile devices?

Yes, our complete course library is optimized for smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

What happens if a student fails a quiz?

Students have up to 3 attempts to pass module check quizzes. Instructors offer real-time assistance if needed.

Do cards expire?

OSHA Outreach cards do not expire, but industry standards require refresher training every 3 to 5 years.
